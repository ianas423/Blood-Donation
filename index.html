Doctor's Advice & Donation Precautions

Essential medical guidelines for before, during, and after blood donation.

1

Before Blood Donation

  • Drink plenty of water or healthy fluids (at least 500ml) prior to donation.
  • Eat a healthy, low-fat meal 2-3 hours before donating. Avoid fasting.
  • Ensure you get a full 7-8 hours of sound sleep the night before.
2

After Blood Donation

  • Rest for 10-15 minutes at the donation site and enjoy refreshments.
  • Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous physical exercise for 24 hours.
  • Keep the bandage on your arm for at least 4 hours and keep it dry.
